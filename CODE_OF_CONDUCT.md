# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The CODSOFT project repository welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to keep project collaboration respectful, constructive, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience and respect.
- Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's contact information or private data.
- Keep project communication relevant and appropriate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Publishing private information without consent or using project channels to target another person is prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove or edit content, close discussions, or restrict participation to address harmful behavior. They should explain moderation decisions when practical and handle reports with care.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the repository owner through [GitHub @brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31). Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Provide links, dates, and context where possible. Reports will be handled as privately as the available GitHub tooling permits.

Maintainers will consider the context, impact, and pattern of behavior when deciding on a response. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
